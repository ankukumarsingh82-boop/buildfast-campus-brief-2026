INDIRA INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY
Office of the Dean (Student Affairs)
Circular No. DSA/2026/091
Date: 18 September 2026

Subject: Mid-semester notices for examinations, fees, hostel, and Aether 2026

1. End-semester examination form
All UG students of batches 2023, 2024, and 2025 must submit the end-semester examination form on the ERP portal by 2 October 2026, 5:00 PM. Forms submitted after this deadline will not be accepted. A late window from 3 October 2026 to 4 October 2026 carries a fine of Rs 500 per day. Students with unpaid tuition will be blocked from the form.

2. Tuition fee
The second installment of tuition fee is due on 30 September 2026. Failure to pay by 6 October 2026 will result in debarment from the end-semester examinations and withholding of the hall ticket.

---PAGE---

3. Hostel allotment
Odd-semester hostel re-allotment opens on 25 September 2026 and closes on 1 October 2026 at 11:59 PM. Students who do not confirm their room on the hostel portal will be treated as vacated. Mess advance of Rs 12000 is payable with confirmation.

4. Tech fest Aether 2026
Team registration for Aether 2026 closes on 28 September 2026. Each team must upload an abstract of at most 400 words and a faculty mentor endorsement. The fest runs 14-16 November 2026. Teams without a mentor letter will be rejected.

---PAGE---

5. Merit scholarship
Applications for the 2026 merit-cum-means scholarship must reach the scholarship cell by 10 October 2026 along with an income certificate and the previous semester grade card. Incomplete applications will not be reviewed.

6. Library clearance
Students appearing for the end-semester exam must obtain library no-dues by 8 October 2026. Hall tickets will not be issued without clearance.

Contact: dean.students@iitcampus.edu.in
